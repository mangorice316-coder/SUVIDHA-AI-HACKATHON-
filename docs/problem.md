## 1. The Core Problem Statement
> *"A multilingual learner may understand a subject concept in a familiar linguistic context, but struggles to connect that understanding to the formal academic terminology and phrasing used by the curriculum. This creates a barrier when they must read, interpret, or respond to subject material in the academic register."*

---

## 2. Empirical Research Foundation
1. **Science Vocabulary Variance (Grade 8 Study):** Science vocabulary knowledge uniquely explains **20% of the variance in science test scores** among English learners after controlling for general reading and background knowledge.
2. **IES / CREATE Research Base:** Shows that disciplinary language combined with content-based vocabulary instruction significantly improves science comprehension for English learners.
3. **Cross-Language Morphological Analysis (2025):** Demonstrates that multilingual students learn academic words more effectively when allowed to anchor concepts in their home language representations.
4. **UNESCO India Education Report (2025):** Emphasizes multilingual education across India's 1,369 languages, recommending the exact progression: *everyday home language $\rightarrow$ regional terminology $\rightarrow$ formal academic/scientific register*.

---

## 3. The 3 Ineffective Traditional Workarounds
- **Workaround 1 (Literal Machine Translation):** Translates words mechanically into vernacular (e.g. *"curl of B"* $\rightarrow$ *"hair curl"*, *"missing term"* $\rightarrow$ *"missing vocabulary word"*), destroying physical causality and leaving the student unable to write an English exam paper.
- **Workaround 2 (Generic AI Simplification):** Dumbs down the lesson into basic text, trapping the learner inside an isolated AI sandbox and never teaching them to read the actual textbook.
- **Workaround 3 (Rote Memorization):** Student memorizes definitions without understanding the physical mechanism, failing when exam questions apply concepts to new problems.

---

## 4. The BRIDGE Transformation

```

[Independent Curriculum Mastery] ◄── [Quick-Check Task] ◄── [Original Sentence Reconnection]
```
